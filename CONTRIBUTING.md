## Quick Summary of the Contribution Flow

1. **Fork** this repository.
2. **Clone** your fork to your computer.
3. Create a new **branch** (e.g. `add-<your-username>-card`).
4. Add your card JSON file inside `assets/cards/<your-username>.json`.
5. **Commit** and **push** your changes.
6. Open a **Pull Request**.

Once accepted, your card will automatically appear on our [Contributors Showcase](https://jaanque.github.io/good-first-issue/)!
